About me

Hi, I'm Hubert. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Tourism and Hospitality, and for more than five years I have worked in a travel agency in Antananarivo, helping people get where they are going: the right flight, the right fare, and a clear answer at the right time.

That daily work taught me three habits I bring to every client. I check the details before they become problems, I reply clearly and quickly, and I follow every request until it is truly finished.

Today I help founders, small teams and busy professionals the same way. Your inbox, calendar and client messages stay under control, and when you travel, I plan the trip and handle the booking.

Here are the services I can provide, all delivered remotely through email and chat.

Virtual Assistant & Travel Assistant

How a request moves

This is how a travel request works. Virtual assistant work is different: we first talk about your needs, then agree on the tasks and how we will work together.

  1. You send the request

    Route, dates, number of travelers and budget. A short email is enough.

  2. I search and compare

    I check fares and fare rules, then send clear options with a recommendation.

  3. I book and confirm

    Once you confirm, I book your trip with the agency and send the itinerary and invoice.

  4. I follow up

    I watch for schedule changes and handle changes or refunds if your plans move.

What I need from you to start

  • Departure city and destination
  • Travel dates, or how flexible they are
  • Number of travelers
  • Passenger names exactly as on the passports
  • Your budget and preferences (baggage, schedule)
  • A contact for urgent changes

I am open to contracts. If you would like to work together, we can talk on WhatsApp or by email.
